# Research portfolio overview — Human–Robot Team Coordination

**Research development prototype | University of Thessaly — HERMES R&D context**

## Research question
When a robot acting in a multi-human team lacks a reliable confirmation, **should it ask for clarification, and which human should it ask?**

The workbench explores uncertainty-aware information seeking for an equipment-delivery scenario in a simulated surgical team. It aims to study trade-offs among information reliability, deadlines and interruption costs.

## Implemented scope
- Scripted, simulated team scenarios and policy comparisons
- Local Python workbench with browser interface
- Auditable decisions, offline regression checks and experiment exports
- Optional, explicitly started LLM-backed experiments subject to user-provided credentials and limits

See [main README](../README.md), [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [research protocol](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL_GR.md), and [scientific limitations](PAPER_RESEARCH_PLAN_GR.md).

## Research limitations
This software **does not control a physical robot**, transcribe real operating-room speech, evaluate real clinicians or establish clinical safety. Synthetic fixtures and repeated development episodes are not evidence of real-world performance. External evaluation and independent scenarios are still needed.

## MSc relevance
Relevant areas include human–robot interaction, probabilistic/uncertainty-aware decision-making, robotics software, reproducibility and responsible assistive systems.
